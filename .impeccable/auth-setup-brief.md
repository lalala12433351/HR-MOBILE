# Surface: Login, register, org creation, workspace setup

Mode: Operate

Audience: new workspace owners and returning staff on desktop, members on phone.

Task: sign in, create an account, name the organisation, then finish workplace setup so the dashboard is ready to operate.

Constraints: preserve auth, OTP, Cognito policy, and setup persistence. No fake offices or people. DESIGN.md Working Studio is the world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Auth and first-run setup are the same working studio as the company wizard — a Quiet Mist canvas and white form — not a marketing split with a dark sapphire wall.

OWN-WORLD: Cool mist `#f3f5f7`, clean sheet white, deep ink, studio navy buttons, measured blue focus, sage done-states. Plus Jakarta Sans. 40px controls, 12px labels, 7px radii. Rail + content; workspace-plan illustration. No gradient brand panel, no module-icon grid.

STORY: Sign in or create an account, verify email, name the organisation, then configure the workplace. Launch leaves a real workspace, not leftover onboarding chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440-wide frame, 300px rail (wordmark, short title, optional stages, plan illustration), content with one task heading and the form. Primary action is the navy 40px button under the fields.

FORM: Established Pixbe Working Studio; setup wizard as reference. Seed: design-md-auth-setup.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
